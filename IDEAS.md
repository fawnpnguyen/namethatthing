# Name That Thing — Idea Bank

A running brainstorm of puzzle ideas. Anyone can add; Fawn and Claude settle the grade.

## How this works

- **Fawn** adds ideas anytime. Her ideas get built first.
- **Muse** pulls from **Ready to build** when choosing a batch's topics (matching the grade and shelf it's filling), and may add new ideas to **Brainstorm** with status `Muse idea`. Muse never moves an idea into Ready to build.
- **Fawn and Claude** move ideas from Brainstorm to Ready to build once the grade is settled.
- When an idea becomes a live puzzle, move it to **Built** with the date.
- Every number listed here is a starting lead, not a sourced fact. The brief's sourcing rules apply when it's built.
