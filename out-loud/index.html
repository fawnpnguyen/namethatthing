NAME THAT THING OUT LOUD

Can you name it in 5?

Preview: 3 sample puzzles

Five clues. Five points down to one. Pick an occasion and the game deals the puzzles.

Games run 5, 10, or 15 puzzles: 10 is the standard game, 5 for a staff meeting. This preview deals from 3 sample puzzles and reshuffles the bank when it runs out, so you can test the whole flow: scoring, the 5-second clock, and the bench, in a few minutes.

Who's playing?

Puzzles in this game: the occasion's usual length is picked for you.

How to host: tap their score card at the top the moment they raise a hand. The card's border is their 5-second clock: thick and full in their color, it drains as time runs out, and an empty ring means a freeze.

Tap ✓ if they name it in time, ✗ if they're wrong. A plain wrong guess costs the points and benches that team for the next clue. Then it's hands-up again for everyone else, at normal risk. Miss on clue 5 and the bench carries over to clue 1 of the next puzzle. If the clock runs out, that's a freeze: it counts as wrong with the full deduction, the same as any wrong guess—then it's hands-up again for everyone else. Scores can go negative: there's no floor.

Sudden death: no bench carries into it and no points are deducted during it. Only a correct guess ends it; if nobody names it, another sudden-death puzzle is dealt.
